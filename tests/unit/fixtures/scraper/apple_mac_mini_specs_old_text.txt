Display Support

M6

Supports up to three external displays using Thunderbolt and HDMI ports:

Three displays: Two displays up to a native resolution of 6K at 60Hz or 4K at 165Hz, plus a third display up to 5K at 60Hz over Thunderbolt or 4K at 60Hz over HDMI Up to two displays: One display up to a native resolution of 8K at 60Hz or 5K at 120Hz or 4K at 240Hz, plus a second display up to 5K at 60Hz over Thunderbolt or 4K at 60Hz over HDMI
Supports up to two external displays over a single Thunderbolt port
Thunderbolt 4 (USB‑C) ports support native DisplayPort 1.4 speeds
Supports variable refresh rate (VRR), HDR and multi‑channel audio

M5 Pro

Supports up to three external displays over any combination of Thunderbolt and HDMI ports:

Three displays up to a native resolution of 6K at 60Hz or 4K at 165Hz Up to two displays: One display up to a native resolution of 8K at 60Hz or 5K at 120Hz or 4K at 240Hz, plus a second display up to 5K at 120Hz or 4K at 200Hz
Supports up to three external displays over a single Thunderbolt port
Thunderbolt 5 (USB‑C) ports support native DisplayPort 2.1 speeds
Supports variable refresh rate (VRR), HDR and multi‑channel audio
